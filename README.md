# generative-ai-market-analysis
# The Generative AI Battle

### A Market, Competitive and Digital Marketing Analysis of OpenAI, Google and Anthropic

## Overview
This project examines how three selected Generative AI competitors attract users, differentiate their offerings and compete through product strategy, digital presence, pricing and customer trust.

## Research Objectives
- Understand key trends in the Generative AI industry.
- Compare the strategies of OpenAI, Google and Anthropic.
- Analyse digital marketing and customer acquisition approaches.
- Examine consumer preferences using primary survey research.
- Develop practical marketing recommendations.

## Methodology
The study combines secondary research from industry reports and official company sources with a consumer survey of 45 respondents.

## Project Contents
- Research report
- Interactive HTML presentation
- Survey findings and charts
- Research methodology and references

## Limitations
The survey is exploratory and is not representative of the entire Indian population. Company-reported statistics and market forecasts are identified as such.
