THE HIDDEN COST OF AI

Every AI interaction has an infrastructure footprint.

Global data-centre electricity consumption grew by approximately 17% in 2025.

~485 TWh 2025
→
~950 TWh 2030

Projected global data-centre electricity demand

AI-focused data centres are among the faster-growing sources of demand. IEA, 2026.

ENERGY

Data centres require substantial electricity, with AI workloads contributing to rising demand.

WATER

Cooling systems can require water, depending on location, climate and cooling technology.

HARDWARE

AI depends on GPUs, servers, networking equipment and semiconductor supply chains.

CARBON

Environmental impact depends partly on electricity sources, efficiency and infrastructure.

AI VALUE Productivity · Research · Accessibility · Automation
↔
AI FOOTPRINT Energy · Water · Hardware · Carbon

THE QUESTION ISN'T WHETHER AI HAS A COST —
IT'S WHETHER EFFICIENCY AND VALUE CAN OUTPACE IT.

Strategic recommendations

THE STRATEGIC PLAY

Three companies. Three different paths to sustainable advantage.

OPENAI 01 / RETENTION

TURN USAGE INTO LOYALTY

Finding

ChatGPT has strong everyday adoption and broad use cases.

Marketing implication

The challenge shifts from acquiring users to deepening retention and professional value.

Recommended actions

  • Personalised workflows
  • Education & professional use
  • Agents and connected tools
  • Reliability + trust
  • Transparent advertising
USAGE→HABIT→LOYALTY
GOOGLE 02 / INTEGRATION

MAKE GEMINI IMPOSSIBLE TO SEPARATE FROM GOOGLE

Finding

Google's biggest advantage is its existing ecosystem.

Marketing implication

AI becomes more powerful when it appears naturally across products users already rely on.

Recommended actions

  • Search
  • Workspace
  • Android
  • YouTube
  • AI-powered discovery & commerce
DISCOVERY→INTEGRATION→HABIT
ANTHROPIC 03 / AUTHORITY

OWN TRUSTED PROFESSIONAL AI

Finding

Anthropic has strong positioning around reasoning, coding, safety and enterprise use.

Marketing implication

It can deepen authority in high-value professional workflows without winning every consumer use case.

Recommended actions

  • Enterprise
  • Coding
  • Research
  • Agents
  • Privacy / trust
  • Cloud partnerships
TRUST→EXPERTISE→ENTERPRISE
